# Quickstart: Events explorer

On a stand serving the test graph (two versions):

1. Open "Events all domains view": Domains mode, only domains, counts as in User Story 1; panel Domains + Coverage, gear shift of two points.
2. Click a domain with a vocabulary: its events on the next layer, an event held by a second domain joined to it too; Events section and a third point appear.
3. Click an event: its actions; Methods section; the actions have no ports but one dashed joining port toward domains; open it: lines to their domains.
4. Switch to Events: every event; open one's domains, then a domain's actions: all its actions, the quiet ones "no events"; Actions section.
5. Switch to Actions: emitting actions only; paint by Methods: strips and frames change before any line is drawn.
6. Move each gear shift point at each step: the screen changes every time.
7. Copy the address, open it in a new tab: same mode, ports, filters, paint; join a room as a follower: the same.
8. Compare the two versions: marks on nodes and lines; a closed port carries "+1" / "−1" in its colours; Side by side shows each version's part.
9. Open a 1.2 document: domains only, no error.
