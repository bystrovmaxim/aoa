# Quickstart: spec 043

On `erd-full` (see the stand in spec.md), with `maxitor-client` and `maxitor-api` running:

1. Hover an unchanged field: after about 300 ms the card shows its name and type; no label.
2. Sweep across the diagram: no card, no `element-card` request.
3. Click any name, changed or not, in One and Diff: copied, no card.
4. Hover `lifecycle` on a table: the miniature; a second hover sends no request; click it: the full diagram.
5. In Diff on a changed lifecycle: coloured states.
6. On the full graph, hover a node, a link, a bundle, a folded domain; click a change: as today.
7. Phone width: tap a field: card with Copy.
8. In a room, follow: the card shows on the follower.
9. Collapse a table: key rows and `N fields hidden`; its header stays put; lines stay on rows; reload keeps it.
10. Collapse all; expand one; the address stays short; expand all.
11. In Diff collapsed: changed rows stay, the stepper reaches them.
