# UI contract: spec 043

- **Card**: `data-testid="element-card"`, 300 px wide, opens after 300 ms, stays while the pointer is on it or its element, closes 200 ms after, on Escape, on a press or a wheel on the diagram.
- **Miniature**: `data-testid="lifecycle-miniature"`, a button.
- **Copy**: `data-testid="card-copy"`, only on a touch screen.
- **Address**: `collapsed=1`, `except=a,b`.
- **Toolbar**: a button named `Collapse all` or `Expand all`.
- **Header control**: cell `a_cl<table>`.
- **Hidden count**: a last row `N fields hidden`.
